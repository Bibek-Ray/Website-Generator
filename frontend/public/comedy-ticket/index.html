Frequently Asked Questions

How do I buy tickets?

Click the 'Get Tickets' button on any show page. Follow the prompts to select your seats and complete your purchase.

Can I choose my seats?

Yes! Our ticketing system allows you to select your preferred seats when available. Check the seating chart during checkout.

What are your refund policies?

All ticket sales are final. However, if a show is canceled, you will receive a full refund.

What time should I arrive?

Doors typically open 30-60 minutes before showtime. Arriving early ensures you have time to find your seats and grab a drink!

Is there a drink minimum?

No, there is no drink minimum. However, we have a fully stocked bar with a great selection of beverages.

What are the age restrictions?

Age restrictions vary by show. Please check the show details for specific age requirements.

Where is the venue located?

The venue address is listed on the show page and in your confirmation email. We also provide a map.
